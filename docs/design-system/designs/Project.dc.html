Project overview

Benin has 63 people groups representing more than 14 million people. Twenty percent of those people groups still need to be reached.

unfoldingWord's Church-Centric Bible Translation partners in Benin are working on five new Open Bible Stories translations. The stories are immediately used in evangelism, and churches working with materials in local languages have seen significant spiritual and numerical growth.
