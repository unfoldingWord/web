Portfolio colors

The work is presented in four portfolios, each with one brand color. On the public site, unrestricted giving is called Equipping Partners, and Strategic Language Resources and Tools and Technology both sit inside Innovation.
Transformation
Church networks equipped to reach the least reached. Why this color. Cultivate. This portfolio is field work: churches planted, leaders developed, disciples made. It is the color of things growing.
#70C9CC #E2F4F5 tint
Ocean or Tech text on the solid color.
Innovation
Open resources and software the church uses to meet its own needs. Why this color. Inspire. It is the primary brand color and the color of the mark, and reads as the most digital of the five. Open content and tools are what unfoldingWord is best known for.
#31ADE3 #D6EFFA tint
Large white headings only on the solid color. Small labels use Inspire ink (#0B6E9E) on white.
Catalyzation
A worldwide movement of church leaders taking ownership of Bible translation. Why this color. Ocean. This portfolio builds a lasting, worldwide network of leaders through forums and coaching, and Ocean is the deepest and steadiest brand color.
#014263 #CCD9E0 tint
White text on the solid color. Inspire, Cultivate or Kindle for emphasis.
Equipping Partners
Families, churches and foundations whose partnership strengthens all of the work. Why this color. Kindle. It is the color of the Give button, so the default ask on every page already points here. This is where-most-needed giving, the portfolio we most want people to choose.
#E59D33 #FAEBD6 tint
Tech or Ocean text on the solid color.
Where the color shows: the hero panel and number band of a portfolio page, its tile in any grid of the four, and its label on stories, projects and giving pages. The Give button is Kindle with Tech text on every page. That matches Equipping Partners on purpose: the default ask always points to where-most-needed giving. Inside a Cultivate, Inspire or Kindle panel a button is Ocean with white text, because Kindle disappears on Kindle and sits poorly on the other two.

From the brand book: the mark, angles and grain

The mark as a supporting shape Large, in full color, behind a person cut out from their photo. It may be cut off at the bottom or sides, but the curved top always shows. Never placed over a face.
Angled color layers Brand colors at 70% opacity falling from the top-left corner, at the brand book's angle. Only on photos where no face sits under the color.
Grainy gradients Subtle gradients in brand colors, always with a fine grain. Used behind hero panels, the give band and the footer.

Emphasis in headlines

The church in every people group.
The Bible in every language.
Key phrases may be set in Inspire or Kindle on Ocean grounds only. On white they fail contrast, so headlines stay Ocean. Headlines are Avenir Black.

Voice checks for every page

The church is the hero Say "Your generosity equips local believers", never "Your donation funds our programs". Lead with church and gospel impact; tools are the means.
Hopeful, never an emergency No fear, guilt or life-or-death urgency, and speed is not the point. Photos show people who look natural and hope-filled, not suffering.
Keep partners safe Stories and the footer carry the line "Names, locations, and images may have been altered or obscured for security purposes." Islands-Asia is always hyphenated.
