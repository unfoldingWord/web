The Cry for the Gospel in Islands-Asia

Islands-Asia has over 700 language groups but only 34 complete Bibles. One partner's ministry now works with 130 language groups using Strategic Language Resources.

With tears in her eyes, the tribal woman stood up in the meeting, slapped her palms on the table, and said, "We could all be gone by the time you get to us!" That moment marked a change in Birhan.

Birhan came to Bible translation from the business world. As a forensic auditor, he was accustomed to digging for facts. His investigation into Bible translation in his country revealed discouraging data.

A demand that traditional methods cannot meet

After 70 years of work by many Bible translation agencies, only 100 New Testaments are complete and only 34 complete Bibles. The demand for Bible translation there far exceeds the capacity of traditional methods.
