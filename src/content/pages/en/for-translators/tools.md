  - title: <span id="translationcore"></span>translationCore
    description: |-
      translationCore® is an [open source](https://github.com/unfoldingWord-dev/translationCore) platform for checking and managing Bible translation projects. tC provides an extensible interface that enables, among other things, systematic and comprehensive checking of Bible translations against multiple sources and the original languages with just-in-time [training modules](/for-translators/training/#UTA) that provide guidelines and instruction for translators.

      [Click here](https://www.translationcore.com) to view translationCore® on the web.
  - title: <span id="translationstudio"></span>translationStudio
    description: |-
      translationStudio (tS) is an [open source](https://github.com/unfoldingWord-dev/ts-desktop/) utility for translating the Bible and biblical content into any language. The purpose of tS is to make Bible translation accessible to the global church. tS is fully functional without an internet connection and enables collaboration in the translation process. The app includes numerous just in time translation helps that explain keywords, difficult to translate phrases, and general translation concepts.

      **Desktop & Mobile**

      The desktop version of tS is available for Windows, Mac, and Linux. The mobile version supports Android devices.

      [Click here](https://forum.door43.org/t/translationstudio-desktop-version-12-1/465) for release notes & downloads.

      **Docs**

      See the following links for help using translationStudio:

      Slack: join our [Door43 Slack](https://door43.org/en/contact/)

      Official documentation: <https://ts-info.readthedocs.io/>

      Discussion Forum: <https://forum.door43.org/c/software/ts-documentation>

  - title: <span id="door43"></span>Door43®
    description: |-
      Door43® is a community of people interested in furthering unrestricted biblical content. The [Door43 Content Service](https://git.door43.org/) provides synchronization between our tools and the [Door43](https://door43.org/) website provides automatic web publishing. Contributors from all over the world use the site to work together to create, translate, and distribute unrestricted discipleship resources.

      - [Click here](https://forum.door43.org/) to browse how-tos and ask questions about Door43 and unrestricted biblical content in the [Door43 Forum](https://forum.door43.org/).
      - [Click here](https://door43.org/en/contact) to chat online with others and get fast answers to simple questions via [Door43 Slack](https://door43.org/en/contact).

  - title: <span id="publishing-blockchain"></span>Publishing Blockchain
    description: |-
      The Publishing Blockchain is a decentralized database of completed resources. The blockchain contains information about these resources as well as digital signatures of people and organizations that approve of them. The information in the database cannot be altered and provides a historical account of work published. The database is ideal for publishers of all sizes and developers to pull resources from to input into their publishing engines. [More information](https://github.com/unfoldingWord-dev/PublishingBlockchain/blob/master/README.md#publishing-blockchain-specification).
