    ## A Message from HR

    Do you love Jesus and want to serve with a passionate, highly innovative team of catalysts to empower the global Church? Then, unfoldingWord just might be the place for you.

    ## Vision and Identity

    unfoldingWord’s exists to see the Church in every People Group and the Bible in Every Language. We catalyze and equip church networks so that they are empowered and successful in reaching the least reached. This is why we come to work, this is our motivation, which we believe will bring glory to God.

    [Statement of Faith](/statement-of-faith/)

    ## Our Culture

    We hold these Core Values as essential to who we are. They don’t describe what we do, they describe how we go about doing what we do; they are a description of our culture, and we aspire to embody them. In no particular order:

    - **Returning to Joy** — Because the joy of the Lord is our strength, we refuse to operate out of fear but will return to joy in all circumstances.
    - **Working Interdependently** — Because we seek scalable solutions, we refuse to operate alone but rely on the exponential power of relationships.
    - **Always Adapting** — Because we seek excellence and effectiveness, we strive to innovate and continuously improve.
    - **Walking in Humility** — Because we are one part of the body of Christ, we serve alongside the global Church to understand and meet their needs.

    #### We believe the global Church is best equipped to complete the Great Commission when local leaders have the tools and training they need to translate Scripture into every language. Our work is fueled by a shared passion for making God’s Word accessible to all.
