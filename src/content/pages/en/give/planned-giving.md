- **A legacy gift** can help unfoldingWord equip churches to reach the unreached with God’s Word long after a one-time donation. These options let you include Church-Centric Bible translation training, tools and open-license resources in your estate or long-term giving. unfoldingWord is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 14-1888977.

&nbsp;

- **Bequest in a will or trust.** Name unfoldingWord to receive a set amount, a percentage of your estate, or what remains after your family is provided for. A bequest can be changed during your lifetime and costs nothing to put in place.

&nbsp;

- **Beneficiary designation.** Name unfoldingWord as a full or partial beneficiary of a retirement account, life insurance policy, or other account. This is often done with a simple form from the account provider and does not require rewriting your will.

&nbsp;

- **Donor-advised fund.** Recommend a grant to unfoldingWord now, or name unfoldingWord in your fund’s succession plan so support for Church-Centric Bible Translation continues. Give through your donor-advised fund using EIN 14-1888977.

&nbsp;

- Please talk with your attorney or financial advisor before you decide. They can help you choose the option that best fits your plans.
