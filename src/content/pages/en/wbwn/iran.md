    Ninety-two million people remain unreached in Iran. About half of Iran’s people speak a language other than the national language, Farsi, at home.

    *Due to banking, governmental, and corporate restrictions, unfoldingWord does not accept online donations for humanitarian relief in Iran. You may give by check to support Whole Bible | Whole Nation.*

    Our Whole Bible, Whole Nation partner, Transform Iran, identified 20 strategic languages in which translated scripture would have the greatest impact on church formation. They have completed unfoldingWord’s Open Bible Stories in all 20, plus two Afghan languages. They are working on the New Testament in five of those languages, even in a war zone.
