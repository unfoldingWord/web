    unfoldingWord® is a nonprofit ministry dedicated to unlocking Bible translation so local church networks can quickly, accurately, and sustainably translate Scripture into their own language. We collaborate with other Bible ministries to provide unrestricted, open-source biblical content, technology tools, and training resources to equip the global church for Bible translation and discipleship.

    It is our desire to demonstrate responsible stewardship through total financial transparency. We are members of the [ECFA (Evangelical Council for Financial Accountability)](https://www.ecfa.org/MemberProfile.aspx?ID=29979), have achieved the [GuideStar Platinum Seal of Transparency](https://www.guidestar.org/profile/14-1888977) and have also received the [100% give With Confidence rating from Ministry Watch](https://db.ministrywatch.com/ministry.php?ein=141888977). Explore the links below to browse through our financial reports and additional financial statements.
