      Church leaders and Bible translators from across Africa, Asia, and the Middle East join us for Global or Regional Church-Centric Bible Translation Forums—a time to worship, learn, train, and equip one another to bring God’s Word to the nations. These men and women serve in places where following Jesus comes with serious persecution, and often where no Western missionary can help.

      The stories that begin at these Church-Centric Bible Translation Forums don’t stay there. They ripple outward—reaching entire nations that have never before heard the name of Jesus.

      Picture it: pastors from Sudan worshipping beside believers from Indonesia and Jordan. Hands raised. Voices lifted. Prayers spoken in dozens of languages. Each one represents a community that will soon hear the gospel in their own tongue. And they all say the same thing when they leave: “We are not alone anymore.”

      This is the Church at work—the Body of Christ strengthening itself to fulfill the Great Commission.
