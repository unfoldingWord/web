Charles Z of Benin has been trying to share the gospel of Christ with his Muslim neighbors since 1999. unfoldingWord equips him and his colleagues with the Strategic Language Resources they need to translate God's Word faster than ever before.

Charles' interest in Bible translation arose the week before he heard of unfoldingWord. He was interpreting at a celebration service for a traditionally translated New Testament; there he learned that it took 20 years to complete the project. "I was really shocked and asked myself, 'How will we reach our people if it takes that long?'" The next week, he heard a presentation by unfoldingWord and realized he had found the answer.

Now he and his team are working on five strategic languages in which Islam has recently made inroads. "Our people are story-crazy people. unfoldingWord® Open Bible Stories overcomes their hostility to the message of Christ. Also, churches working with unfoldingWord materials in local languages have experienced significant spiritual and numerical growth."
