"We know that the Bible in the heart language is the key to reaching the unreached," said our South Asian partner, Barnabas. "But a major scholar said it would take 40 years and cost hundreds of thousands of dollars! That seemed impossible to us."\
But gifts from people like you changed the equation. These ministry partners have made significant progress on six New Testament translations since 2022. Their ministry shepherds over 50,000 believers in house churches and uses every chapter they translate right away. After training with unfoldingWord, Barnabas said, "We learned that it's not impossible! It's possible!"\
Multiplication of disciples, leaders, and churches is the key to reaching the 80 million unreached people in Barnabas' part of South Asia. God's Word in their heart language is their most important tool. We are deeply grateful for the visionary financial partners who are making this possible!
