"Wow," said the pastor/translator. "I've studied this passage for years in the national language, but I never really understood it until I read it like this in my language! Thank you!"

Every time we interview someone newly involved in Church-Centric Bible Translation using the tools we provide, we hear the same story: The translation Book Packages for each book of the Bible open up their understanding of God's Word in ways they never dreamed of. Tears of gratitude often follow.

In every part of the world where we work, the reaction to unfoldingWord's Strategic Language Resources is like that of the disciples who met the risen Christ on the Emmaus road, "They asked each other, 'Were not our hearts burning within us while he talked with us on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?'"

We praise God for the risen Christ and bless him daily for the prayers, vision, and faith of our financial partners who share the goal of seeing the Church in Every People Group and the Bible in Every Language.
