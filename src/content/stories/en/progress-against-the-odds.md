In southern India, Hindu nationalists persecute Christian leaders. Families drive from their homes and villages their sons and daughters who convert to Christianity. Courageous evangelists continue preaching the gospel from Hindi versions of the Bible, but they face a problem. "People can understand most of the words," they said, "but their hearts are not touched by the Bible in Hindi. Our people need the Bible in their heart language."

Armed with unfoldingWord's Bible translation tools, Paul and his team have translated the New Testament for 104 different people groups in Karnataka. "We were able to do it in two years," he says. "unfoldingWord's tCore software helps us be accurate."

To paraphrase Winston Churchill, never in the field of Bible translation have so few been able to do so much, so well, for so many.
